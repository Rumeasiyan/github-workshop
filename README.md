# GitHub workshop

One day, beginner to pro, for the office team. Everything today happens on **GitHub** and in **VS Code**: no paper, no other apps.

**The story:** your team is planning the **office day trip to the beach**. The plan lives in your team's repository, **trip-team-a** or **trip-team-b**: the itinerary, the lunch menu, the packing list and the beach games. Some of it is broken on purpose. By the end of the day your team publishes **the final trip plan v1.0.0**.

## How today works on GitHub

| You want to | Where |
|---|---|
| Know what to do | [MISSIONS.md](MISSIONS.md), and your **mission issues** here (Issues > Assigned to me) |
| Ask for help | Comment on your mission issue and add the `help` label, or post in **Discussions > Q&A** |
| Show you're done | Tick the boxes in your mission issue and comment the proof link |
| See the scores | The **Projects** tab > *Workshop scoreboard* |
| Hear announcements ("URGENT", hints, the timer) | **Discussions > Announcements**: watch your notifications (the bell, top right) |
| Look up a command | [CHEATSHEET.md](CHEATSHEET.md) |

## Rules for today

- Work only in **this repository** and **your team's trip repository**. Don't touch TOME, ASGARD or anything else.
- Everything here is invented. No real names (other than yours), no passwords, no real data.
- **Mistakes are the point.** Break things here; that's what it's for.
- Points (XP) are for fun. The Office decides whether any of it counts in the league.
